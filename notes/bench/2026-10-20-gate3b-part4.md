## F-p — a take loaded into a playing pad keeps the old window (new)

## F-o — the analog outputs crunch, USB stays clean

Twice on 20/10 (~19:12, and ~21:40 while Thomas fine-tuned 3b-18's Slip), both on 37.4.0d: every analog output
(phones, Out 1, the metronome on Out 2) grows progressively into a hard bit-reduction sound, the metronome alone
too. The USB capture of the same outputs stays clean (corr 0.9994 against an earlier good take; Thomas by ear).
Transport stop / start does not cure it; a power cycle does. No fault or watchdog record. One repro try (a
screenshot while he listened) failed.

The USB tap copies the very float buffers stock hands to the float → codec conversion, so the fault is after them.
The whole audio object (`0x2400A9C0`, 37 632 B) dumped twice while crunchy and twice after the cure
(`part3/fo2-audioobj-full-*.txt`): about 2 000 words differ, all integer bookkeeping a boot resets (the sample-cache
table, slot ids, stamps). No float differs; the output trims are identical; no moving word drifts in magnitude. The
crunch is not held in the audio object: it is in the conversion's own state elsewhere, the SAI / DMA link, the codec,
or the analog side.

The second cure was not a decision: mgr-ran went to sleep and its port cut the unit's power (boot record #1, the
backup SRAM wiped, RSR POR + BOR + PIN). So the unit runs on the PC's USB power, and both sightings came late in long
sittings, on the build that adds Munchi's DSP. **Next sittings go through a self-powered USB hub.** F-o never
returns there: power, and `feat/munchi-bank2` (held for F-o) can go. It returns: firmware, and the next step is plain
rc4, then 37.4.0d with Munchi off, and a dev peek that allows the SAI status registers.

## Thomas's design notes from 3b-18

1. « fit auto doesnt make the loop metronome syncable »: on a song cut by ear Auto took the nearest whole number of
   beats, not bars, and he set 2 bars by hand. That is Auto's rule since his own 16/10 answer (feature doc §3, Fit:
   « that weird length is intended as polyrhythmic loop and should be handled properly »). His call: keep Auto
   polymetric, make it the nearest length in Fit's list, or add a bar-locked choice beside it.
2. While TL / TR move, the overview should show the region's bracket instead of following the playhead, and follow
   it again after a pause.
