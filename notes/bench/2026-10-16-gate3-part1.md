## Findings (→ the fix batch, hub #t-021 / #t-022 / #t-023)

1. **Slip knob** — the 150 ms detent is short at slow speed (overshoots 0); the 1 → 10 → 60 tiers still feel stepped.
   The speed log (`bbusb.py knob 90`, `/data/dev/gate3/knob-log-1.txt`): events every ~13 ms; median counts per event
   by speed 1–6: 21, 35, 45, 63, 80, 116; Thomas's fast turns reach speed 6 easily (302 of 1 212 events).
2. **Waveform knob labels** ("Loop Start", "Loop End", "Slip", "Loop Len") are too long and overlap; the values are
   hidden.
3. **A WAV's Beat Count overrides the region**: a Resam'd 2-bar take carries Beat Count 8; on a 1-bar region the clip
   counts 8 beats (fps = region / n), so stock re-places the playhead every step (a stutter, and the playhead display
   jumps). Beat Count Auto / 4 fixes it. Thomas wants the loop *fitted* to the closest / a chosen beat count → **Fit**
   (#t-022; a true pitch-keeping stretch → P6).
4. A region that is not a whole number of beats stutters at every Sync step (Mk1 Clip mode never stretches) — Fit.
5. **A region grown from under half a beat is held**: the 0-beat region has no grid, so the wrap-grid hold waits for a
   wrap that never comes; stop / play needed.
6. **Hard splice at the region seam**: a single-sample step of 0.133 / 0.206 at every wrap (typical max 0.019 per
   50 ms): the fold reads Loop End → Loop Start, stock's crossfade blends two identical streams → **honour the pad's
   Loop Fade** on a Region (stock's formula, #t-022).
7. **Region Auto needs Loop Mode** (a Sample-mode setting): a fresh take with moved loop points stays on File, no
   bracket. Auto should follow the loop points in Clip mode.
8. **Loop Len's in-point is taken at the knob's first value** (Off → 1/16 → … → 1/4): a 1/4 loop starts on a 1/16
   boundary and forces Sync 1/16. Take the in-point once the value settles.
9. **A slot past the file's end after the browser hands the pad its file back**: slot 0 active at pos 315 848 (a
   336 359-frame browsed file) on B's 192 000-frame file, never advancing; slot 1 restarting step 0 every 2 beats;
   silence / a 2-beat fragment until stop / play. Also at Slip 0 (our hooks pass through there): stock's handling, made
   recoverable by ending such slots on a file change.
10. **Load peak 99 %** during the stuck episode (24/24 % after a clean browse): re-check after 9.
