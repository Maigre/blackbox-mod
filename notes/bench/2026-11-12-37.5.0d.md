## Not proven here

- By ear: ChopShop as on JOEBOX 2, a region and a Fit case, Trim (#t-041), Region Move (#t-043), the white playhead
  line (#t-049), the Fit row's order with Thomas's presets (#t-048).
- A Clip pad's sound with bank 2 held off: with the transport stopped a pad triggered by MIDI only arms (stock), and
  nothing but a hand can press PLAY while the kit is off with the rest of bank 2.
- « !held » on TOOLS: the same reason, nothing reaches the TOOLS button while held. The flag was read in RAM.
- A real fault and a real watchdog reset adding to the count (the recorder's own token and flush): the token was
  written by the poke here. The suites cover the path; the unit has not crashed to show it.
- Whether an install erases bank 2 (`notes/re/x7` §9): this one changed the payload, so its rewrite says nothing.
  One BACK + INFO with the card as it is now would.
- The kit's jobs not run: cancel, a held button, a large file, a long knob job.

## For the next installs

The card no longer has to travel: `bbkit.py put --replace` writes both files over USB (the kit is in bank 2, so
from a running 37.5.0d or later), and BACK + INFO at power-on is the only thing left to a hand.
