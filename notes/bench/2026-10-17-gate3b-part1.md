## Findings

- **F-a** Pad 1 (whole file, Fit Auto, Slip −0.645): a click once a pass. Not the Slip fold (mid-step, clean on this
  take, whose ends meet): the slipped pass's stock wrap, step 55 → step 0, where for 180 samples the content plays
  twice (0.193 against 0.099), as if step 0 entered at full gain while step 55 faded out. Unicorn does not reproduce
  it (step 0 fades in from 0 at every wrap in every variant tried). Next: pad 1's slots peeked around a slipped wrap
  (needs the transport running: an unattended probe on 17/10 found it stopped — a launch quantized to a bar waits).
- **F-b** TR (Loop End) is hard to set exactly: "not enough accel and hard to reach precise value (need extreme
  zooming)" (Thomas). For 3b-11.
- **F-d** The Waveform's corner labels are hard to read over the waveform: FUN_080a5b90 sets the view's four knobs to
  palette 9 (#666666) over a cyan #09D7F5 waveform (3.3:1) and a #141414 background (3.2:1); white would be 1.7:1 on the
  cyan. Thomas: keep the 2× font and the compact names, white with a 1 px black outline, in every pad mode → 37.3.1.
- **F-e** The Waveform playhead "loops before the end marker": stock's region-relative position drawn from frame 0
  (6 072 frames left of the audio on B, Slip ignored; H5 was never built). Thomas: fix → 37.3.1 (H5).
- **F-f** Loop Len 1/4: every 8 beats (the clip's wrap) the window's first 256 samples differ by up to 0.18; the same on
  Gate 3's 37.2.x take. Thomas: no audible tick → no change.
- **F-g** Loop Len's settle (~170 ms) took 1/8 on an ordinary turn (a 216 ms rest). Thomas: a longer settle → 37.3.1
  (7 polls, 299–341 ms). The overshoots of fast corrections (4 bars, 1 bar, 1/2) were real rests of 387–693 ms and stay
  taken by any settle that still feels responsive.
- **F-h** Loop Len stepped up from 1/16 keeps the 1/16's in-point (1 440, 4 320), so 1/4 holds Sync forced at 1/8.
  Thomas: "Traktor style" — the in-point is kept on a resize: as designed.
- **F-i** During the tempo check (Fit 1 bar, Loop Fade 5 %, 126 BPM) the unit's USB went quiet once: an audio read
  error and a control timeout at the same moment, no restart, no fault record; afterwards the debug peek answered one
  request late. 37.3.1d flushes EP0 IN on a new SETUP (the stale reply stayed queued in TX FIFO 0).
- **F-j** A bench over-read (64 bytes at the boot record, `0x38800FD8`) read `0x38801000`, past backup SRAM, and bus-
  faulted the unit (PC 0x080fd0b8, CFSR 0x8200, BFAR 0x38801000; one SOFTWARE reset). The DEBUG peek had no bounds;
  37.3.1d bounds peek and poke to whole regions. The bench tool's fault, dev builds only.

## Tools added during the gate (fork-only)

- `bbshot.py`: a screenshot over the peek — LTDC 320 × 240, two ARGB1555 layers (`0xC0025B00`, `0xC004B500`, pitch
  640, 240 lines), blended as the LTDC does (BFCR 0x607, BCCR #141414); 13 s a shot.
- `bbmidi.py`: notes, a drift-free 24 ppqn clock, raw bytes over `/dev/snd/midiC2D0`. Pad Note "Default" = note
  36 + q on the Global In channel (1); a note toggles its pad at its Quant point. MIDI Start does not start the
  transport on this unit's settings.
- 37.3.1d (`halo`): the bounded peek / poke, the stale-reply flush, and a parameter mailbox (`bbusb.py set` / `get`)
  served after the knob scan through the app's own `0x6E` / SetCellParam path — not yet on the unit.
