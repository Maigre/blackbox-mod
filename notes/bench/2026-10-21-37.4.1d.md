## F-n — a truncated copy on the card, not the firmware

Loading RC000032 into a pad (pad 1, a playing Clip pad; then pad 9, a stopped Sample pad) played a first chunk and then
silence, looping that way; RC000025 into pad 1 once played one pass and stopped. A cure (the same file loaded again)
seemed to work, and a lead (the file also sits on pad 5, with a Clip Window region) fitted the first sightings.

`bench/bbwaves.py`, new, reads stock's PCM cache (`notes/re/x2-sdram-and-memory.md` §2) per wave: which 8192-frame
blocks are in memory and whether they hold sound. Pad 1's and pad 9's RC000032 each got a new wave with every block
allocated (101, 102) and sound in blocks 0..18 only; pad 5's RC000032 is whole (99 blocks). The wave records carry the
path: the cut ones are `Presets\SYNTH B\RC000032.wav` (preset B's folder), pad 5's is `\Presets\SYNTH\RC000032.wav`.
The record's `+0x004` is the FatFs FIL (this build: 64-bit sizes, `objsize` at `+0x10`): the SYNTH B copy is
**903 168 B** on the card, the original 4 833 890 B, and both headers promise 805 563 frames. The loader trusts the
header, allocates the whole length, reads to the file's end (150 442 frames, the 19 blocks) and leaves zeros, with no
error and no SD miss (pad 9's load: none).

So these sightings are a damaged file. Pad 5, the Clip Window and « another pad holds it » were coincidences; streamed
files resting with a 3-block head (RC000005, a 768 000-frame loop) are stock's normal streaming (pad 15 started: 3 → 23
blocks, all sound). Open: how preset B's Save wrote a short copy — a power loss on mgr-ran's USB power, stock's copy, or
something in the card task's loop (b2load runs in front of each file command). `bench/bbcard.py CARD_ROOT` lists every
WAV whose header promises more than the file holds. The earlier F-n sightings (19-20/10, RC000005 on pad 1) predate the
probe; a scan of the card says whether a copy of RC000005 is short too.
